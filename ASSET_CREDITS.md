`team-campus-night-display-v3.webp`, `team-campus-walk-display-v3.webp`, `team-tree-a01-display-v3.webp`, `team-tree-a02-display-v3.webp` and `team-tree-a03-display-v3.webp` are compressed display copies of campus photographs supplied by the team. `team-tree-a01-blank-plaque-v4.webp` adds a virtual blank wooden plaque to the A01 photograph with AI assistance. Names are rendered dynamically by the game. This is a concept preview. The memorial plaque already visible in the original A03 photograph predates this project and is not evidence of an installation by this team.

Classroom discussion photographs containing students are not included in the public repository.

## Original illustrated species

`fish-tang-*`, `fish-angel-*` and `fish-tetra-*` are original vector illustrations created for Campus Reef with Codex coding assistance. They depict Yellow tang, Silver angelfish and Neon tetra as stylised game characters. The editable SVG files are in `assets/illustrations/`; their transparent WebP renderings are in `assets/images/`. Each species has natural, sailor and scarf versions. They are game artwork rather than photographic evidence.

The four swimming companions are labelled local demo characters. Baby fish reuse the selected species artwork at a smaller scale as a game growth reward. These visuals do not imply other online players, biological simulation or a shared server.

Its [licence file](https://github.com/Eren-Ozcan/reefy/blob/master/LICENSE) does not grant reuse of its code or game assets. This project did not copy its code, images, icons, font files or audio. The water scene, plants, rocks, driftwood, icons and sound were independently implemented; audio is synthesised in the browser.
